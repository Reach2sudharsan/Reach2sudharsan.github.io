02 / Research

Questions I’m exploring

My interests span the algorithms and embodied systems that help machines understand and navigate the world.

01

Depth Estimation Ensembles & Perception Fusion

Lab for CATS · Harvey Mudd College · Sept 2026–Present

Investigating how to combine complementary depth estimates from convolutional neural networks, vision transformers, diffusion models, and stereo geometry. Adapting monocular-stereo refinement methods toward model-agnostic fusion across different depth sources.

02

Uncertainty Quantification & Autonomous Navigation

ML and Autonomy for Diverse Domains Lab · Jan–Sept 2026

Developed rtCP, a conformal prediction framework for uncertainty-aware UAV semantic segmentation using CLIPSeg. Class-frequency-aware thresholds address class imbalance; the method achieved at least 90% per-class coverage for most evaluated classes across two UAV image datasets.

03

Computer Vision & Synthetic Data

ML and Autonomy for Diverse Domains Lab · 2025

Curated wildfire simulation datasets with text-to-video foundation models for NeRF-based 3D scene reconstruction, and developed video-generation benchmarks. Also built synthetic Mars terrain data with Stable Diffusion and ControlNet, then fine-tuned YOLOv11 for crater detection.

04

Signal Processing

Music Information Retrieval Lab · May 2024–Dec 2024

Proposed Sequential FlexDTW, a dynamic time-warping approach with linear time complexity, fixed memory, and flexible local boundary alignment for music synchronization. Presented this work at International Audio Laboratories in Erlangen, Germany.
