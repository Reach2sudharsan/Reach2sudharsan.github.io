03 / Research

Research experience

I develop and evaluate methods for perception in challenging environments, with an emphasis on uncertainty quantification and combining complementary sources of information.

01

Depth Estimation Ensembles & Perception Fusion

Lab for CATS · Harvey Mudd College · Sept 2026–Present

Investigating how to combine depth representations from convolutional networks, vision transformers, latent diffusion models, and stereo geometry. Adapting monocular–stereo mutual-refinement methods toward model-agnostic fusion across diverse depth sources.

Research question: How can complementary geometric and semantic depth cues be fused across different model architectures?

02

Uncertainty Quantification & Autonomous Navigation

ML and Autonomy for Diverse Domains Lab · Jan–Sept 2026

Developed rtCP, a conformal prediction framework for uncertainty-aware UAV semantic segmentation using CLIPSeg. It prododuces class-frequency-aware thresholds to address class imbalance. Evaluated against MC Dropout, regular split conformal prediction, and a per-class conformal baseline across two UAV image datasets, rtCP achieved at least 90% coverage for most object classes at a 90% target.

Outcome: First-author paper in ION GNSS+ 2026 conference proceedings; poster presented at Stanford ACT4Aero conference 2026.

03

Computer Vision & Synthetic Data

ML and Autonomy for Diverse Domains Lab · 2025

Curated wildfire simulation datasets using test-to-video (T2V) foundation models, enabling NeRF-based 3D scene reconstruction for autonomous drone navigation; developed novel benchmarks and evaluation protocols for T2V video outputs. Built synthetic terrain datasets using Stable Diffusion and ControlNet with Mars DEMS; fine-tuned YOLOv11 for crater detection.

04

Signal Processing

Music Information Retrieval Lab · May 2024–Dec 2024

Proposed Sequential FlexDTW, a dynamic time warping approach with linear time complexity, fixed memory, and flexible local boundary alignment. It outperformed Online Time Warping (OTW) and matched or exceeded MrMsDTW and FastDTW across music-synchronization scenarios. Presented at International Audio Laboratories in Erlangen, Germany.

06 / Projects

Selected projects

July–August 2026 · PyTorch, PPO, Gymnasium

Diffusion Policy for Pendulum Control

Built an imitiation-learning pipeline for balancing an inverted pendulum (simulated in MuJoCo) by training a PPO reinforcement learning policy. Generated 50,000 state-action pairs for downstream policy distillation. Implemented a diffusion policy model in PyTorch to clone the PPO policy's behavior from recorded observations.

March–May 2026 · PyTorch, CLIP, CLIPSeg, OpenCV

Enhanced Neural Style Transfer

Built a prompt-guided style-transfer pipeline that aligns semantic image regions and applies region-aware style losses to reduce texture bleeding, with color-preservation applied during post-processing.

December 2025–January 2026 · FAISS, LangChain, Hugging Face

LitPilot

Developed a literature retrieval and recommendation pipeline that indexes arXiv abstracts with FAISS and uses Qwen2.5 to classify retrieved papers by relevance to a user-fed research intent (i.e. "I want papers about Transformer models.").

January–May 2025 · PyTorch, OpenCV, YOLO-Pose

PoseLock

Developed a computer vision pipeline using YOLO-Pose to extract 17-body keypoints from walking videos and construct 10-D kinematic gait embeddings for biometric identification. Engineered gait dynamics features (e.g. shoulder motion) and trained SVM, Random Forest, and XGBoost classifiers with cross-validation, achieving 92% person-identification accuracy.

December 2024–February 2025 · Python, YOLOv5

Vision-based Vehicle Detection Pipeline

Curated and annotated SimTrafficView5K, a custom 5,000-image multi-class dataset with vehicles generated from a simulated 2D traffic intersection. Fine-tuned a YOLOv5s model to achieve multi-class vehicle identification.
